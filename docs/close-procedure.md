## Food counts — open, 86, close

### At open
GMs enter **starting qty in Toast inventory** for every food SKU they count (Main Street Bistro, Lisa Cordero tacos, Sysco food, pastry, etc.). Those items stay on Toast **QUANTITY** tracking through the day.

Do **not** dump tacos or food into the logbook syrup / coffee inventory catalog. That catalog is a different count.

### During the day
When a food qty hits **0**, Toast marks the item **sold out / 86**. BrewShift records the **86 time**.

**Last-sold time** (from Toast orders) is the check — not a substitute. Owen sees **both** times together.

If someone 86s an item in POS before qty hits 0, Toast still fires sold-out. Same 86 time.

### At close
On the **logbook Closing Checklist**, staff enter **leftover qty** and **waste qty** on the **food list** (the Toast QUANTITY food SKUs).

- Leftover = still good, going back / holding for tomorrow
- Waste = discarded at close

This is **not** Sortly and **not** the 31-syrup inventory. It lives on the closing protocol in BrewShift.

Owen uses leftover + waste + 86 time + last-sold time to order food right (not too much, not too little).

## Do not

- Do not invent leftover or waste numbers if Toast stock is unavailable
- Do not skip leftover + waste because the item 86’d earlier — leftover is 0, waste is whatever you discarded
- Do not put drink / syrup qty on this food list
